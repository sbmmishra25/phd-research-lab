# Cross-Project Research Questions

1. Under what data regimes can quantum representations provide measurable utility without claiming quantum advantage?
2. How should noise and finite sampling be incorporated into QML evaluation?
3. Can trustworthy-QML methods quantify uncertainty rather than only maximize accuracy?
4. Can explicit agent planning and audit trails improve reproducibility?
5. How can retrieval systems measure evidence coverage and unsupported generation?
6. What parameter-efficient adaptation regimes provide useful quality/compute trade-offs?
7. How can code-mixed NLP represent language switching without erasing linguistic structure?
8. How should multimodal systems behave when one modality is noisy or absent?
9. Which representations transfer across machines and operating regimes?
