# Research Roadmap

### Phase 1
Quantum fundamentals, optimization, statistical learning and reproducible experimentation.

### Phase 2
Quantum kernels, variational circuits, hybrid QML and noise-aware benchmarking.

### Phase 3
Trustworthy AI, uncertainty, calibration, robustness and explainability.

### Phase 4
Agentic AI, retrieval, grounded generation and efficient LLM adaptation.

### Phase 5
Multimodal AI, industrial AI and cross-domain transfer.
