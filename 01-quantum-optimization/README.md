# 01 — Quantum-Enhanced Optimization

## Objective
Study QAOA-style formulations for small combinatorial optimization problems with transparent classical references.

## Research Questions
- How does performance change with circuit depth?
- How sensitive are solutions to initialization and optimizer settings?
- How does the quantum formulation compare with exact enumeration on small instances?

## Metrics
Objective value, approximation ratio, runtime, circuit depth and trainable parameters.

## Extension
Add QAOA implementations, shot noise, hardware noise, multiple graph families and multi-seed statistical analysis.
