# PhD Research Lab

A reproducible research portfolio containing ten PhD-level AI/ML and Quantum AI research projects.

## Research Portfolio

| # | Project | Research Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Quantum-Enhanced Optimization | QAOA-inspired combinatorial optimization |
| 02 | Quantum Kernel Learning | Quantum feature maps and kernel classification |
| 03 | Trustworthy Quantum ML | Robustness, uncertainty and noise sensitivity |
| 04 | Hybrid Quantum-Classical NN | Hybrid differentiable learning |
| 05 | Agentic Decision Intelligence | Planning, tools, memory and auditability |
| 06 | Research RAG | Retrieval, grounding and citation-aware QA |
| 07 | Efficient LLM Adaptation | Parameter-efficient adaptation |
| 08 | Hindi-English Code-Mixed NLP | Hinglish NLP and code-mixing |
| 09 | Multimodal Research Assistant | Vision-language fusion |
| 10 | Predictive Maintenance | Cross-machine transfer and anomaly prediction |

## Research Standards

Each project is designed around research questions, reproducible baselines, evaluation protocols, ablations, computational-cost reporting, limitations and future work.

**No fabricated experimental results are included.** Numerical results should be generated by executing the supplied experiments.
